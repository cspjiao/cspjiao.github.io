Intelligent Application Scenarios

Intelligent Application Scenarios focus on translating graph representation and trustworthiness capabilities into engineering practices that address industry pain points, serving as the core foundation for driving digital transformation. In the field of Financial Security, it enables precise anti-money laundering detection and risk warning through relational pattern mining. In terms of System Security, it achieves real-time vulnerability detection and self-healing by modeling complex software or network topologies. In Social Security scenarios, the technology supports public opinion control, public health monitoring, and urban governance optimization by analyzing large-scale social network interactions. By bridging physical associations with digital reasoning, graph intelligence is comprehensively empowering the construction of security systems in modern society.
